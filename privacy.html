Tri-X Gym Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026

Overview

Tri-X Gym respects user privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This page should be reviewed and updated to match the final behavior of the mobile application before publication.

Information We Collect

Depending on the final app features, information may include contact details, account information, membership records, coaching-related data, and app usage data needed to deliver services and support.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate the app, manage memberships, deliver coaching support, respond to inquiries, improve the user experience, and maintain service quality and security.

Data Sharing

Tri-X Gym does not sell personal information. Data may be shared with trusted service providers only when necessary to operate the app, provide support, or meet legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact privacy@tri-xgym.com.
